# DealFlow360 — End-to-End Demo Script

## Primary Flow

`Quote → Pricing → Margin → Policy Evaluation → Risk → Approval → Customer Negotiation → Re-risk/Reapproval → Fulfillment → Billing → Control Tower`

1. Create a quote as Sales Rep.
2. Observe automatic risk evaluation & manager approval requirement.
3. Approve quote as Sales Manager.
4. Share customer portal link & submit customer counteroffer.
5. Observe approval supersession & fresh risk evaluation.
6. Plan multi-warehouse fulfillment.
7. Generate billing schedule (one-time & recurring).
8. View metrics & deal risk alerts in Control Tower.
