# DealFlow360 — Business Rules & Governance Policy

## Principles

1. **Governance is the Differentiator**: Every risk score or approval requirement must be explainable with policy violations and allowed thresholds.
2. **Backend is Commercial Authority**: Frontend calculations are non-authoritative.
3. **Every Governed Mutation Re-evaluates**: Price/discount changes trigger full re-evaluation (`Pricing → Margin → Policy → Risk → Approvals`).
4. **Approvals Belong to Commercial State**: Any material commercial change supersedes previous approvals.
5. **Truthful Inventory**: Fulfillment cannot exceed available inventory.
